# 🚀 AR Business Card 

An interactive **Augmented Reality (AR) Business Card** that transforms a traditional professional business card into an immersive digital experience. Scan the QR code, open the AR experience, and interact with professional contact links through touch or voice commands.


## ✨ Features

- **Augmented Reality Experience** — View an interactive digital business card through your device's camera.
- **Voice Commands** — Use the microphone button to open contact links by speaking commands.
- **Interactive Contact Icons** — Tap icons to access the corresponding services.
- **Email Integration** — Quickly access email using Gmail.
- **LinkedIn Profile** — Connect professionally through LinkedIn.
- **GitHub Profile** — Explore projects and repositories.
- **Direct Calling** — Initiate a phone call from a compatible device.
- **Resume Access** — Open the professional resume.
- **QR Code Access** — Launch the hosted experience by scanning a QR code.

## 🎙️ Voice Commands

Tap the microphone button inside the AR experience and try these commands:

| Action | Example Voice Commands |
|---|---|
| Email | "Email" or "Open Email" |
| LinkedIn | "LinkedIn" or "Open LinkedIn" |
| GitHub | "GitHub" or "Open GitHub" |
| Call | "Call" or "Phone" |
| Resume | "Resume", "CV", or "Open Resume" |

Touch interaction is also supported through the corresponding icons.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the [Live Demo](https://ar-business-card-4aex.onrender.com) on a compatible device.
2. Allow camera access when prompted.
3. View the AR business card.
4. Tap any contact icon or use the microphone button.
5. Speak a supported voice command to open the corresponding destination.

**Note:** Camera and microphone permissions, browser compatibility, and an internet connection may be required for the full experience.
